## **BUiD Strategy 2025-2030**

## **Introduction **

The British University in Dubai (BUiD), as a non-for-profit leading research-based university in the UAE and Arab Region, has been established in 2003 under the patronage of HH Maktoum Bin Rashid Al Maktoum, the Former Ruler of Dubai. By implementing the education and scientific research systems applied by the distinguished British universities alliance, the University has a rich history of academic excellence, impactful research, innovation and social responsibility that contributed to raising the higher education standard in Dubai Emirate and the UAE. Guided by its mission to empower students and drive impactful research and innovation, the University strive to be a globally connected and recognized committed to fostering a sustainable future through dynamic and inclusive learning environments. Our diverse student body benefits from a wide range of scholarships, educational programs and research initiatives focused on educational, scientific, legal, business, and sociological research. This strategy outlines how BUiD will continue to build upon its strengths and realize its ambitious vision. Through the implementation of this strategy, the University will position itself as a leading knowledge hub for worldclass education, sustainable development, and transformative research, contributing significantly to the UAE's future.

*"**To be a globally connected and recognised university through academic excellence and impactful * *scholarly activities.**"*

- 1. Student-Centeredness: Prioritise the needs and success of students by providing a supportive and enriching educational experience.
- 2. Diversity and Inclusion: Create an inclusive environment that respects and values diversity in all its forms, ensuring equal opportunities for all members of the university community.
- 3. Research, Innovation and Entrepreneurship: Foster a culture of creativity, innovation, and

- 4. Excellence: Commit to the highest national and international standards of academic, administrative, and research excellence.
- 5. Sustainability: Promote environmental stewardship and sustainable practices in all university operations and curricula.
- 6. Integrity: Uphold the highest standards of integrity and transparency in all activities and decisions.

## **5** **|** **STRATEGIC GOALS**

- 3. Leverage Technology for Innovative Learning by utilise advanced tools/practices to enhance the learning experience and operational efficiency.
- 4. Drive Transformative and Impactful Research by establishing BUiD as a leader in research that addresses local, national, and international issues.
- 5. Foster Innovation and Entrepreneurship through cultivating a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship among students, faculty, and staff.
